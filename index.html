About Campus Grievance Redressal Cell

Campus Voice is an official institutional grievance management platform designed to ensure a responsive, fair, and accountable campus environment. Students can easily report equipment failures, sanitation problems, transport irregularities, or administrative challenges with complete transparency.

⏱️ Fast Response

Average review turnaround within 24 to 48 hours by dedicated maintenance staff.

🔒 Data Privacy

Complaints are strictly accessible only by the student who filed them and authorized deans.

📝 Direct Remarks

Receive written action notes and work order updates directly from department heads.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I track my complaint?
Log in to your student account, go to "My Complaints", and click "View" on any ticket to see the real-time progress timeline and admin remarks.
❓ Can other students see my complaints?
No. The system enforces strict backend authorization. Each student can only view, search, and track their own submitted grievances.
❓ How do I register a new account?
Click the "Register" button in the top navigation or on the hero section. Enter your name, college email, and password to create an account immediately.
❓ What should I do for urgent emergencies?
When filing your complaint, set the Priority Level to "Urgent". Urgent tickets are highlighted with high priority on the admin dashboard.
